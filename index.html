Meet The Founder

Sarika Sharma

Founder & Creative Director

"Some journeys begin with stories. Mine began behind them."

My journey began in the world of media operations. I had the opportunity to work with Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd., Star Network (Star India Pvt. Ltd.), and PlanetCast, gaining rich experience in broadcasting operations, content ecosystems, and HR operations. These experiences gave me a deep understanding of how stories are managed, delivered, and experienced by millions.

Over the years, I found myself drawn to the creative side of storytelling—the voices behind the stories, the cultures behind the languages, and the emotions that connect audiences across borders.

That passion led me to content localization, voice production, and creative development—discovering the joy of adapting stories for new audiences without losing their essence. Today, EchoBridge Studio is the reflection of everything I've learned and loved—operations, creativity, technology, and the timeless power of storytelling.
